## Comparison of larval-adult viability between ATPa genotypes on milkweeds of different cardenolide defenses

bioassay_data_analysis_FINAL.Rmd analyzes larval-to-adult viability of engineered Drosophila melanogaster ATPα genotypes reared on control, lettuce, and milkweed-supplemented diets. Survival was modeled as the number of adults emerging out of 30 larvae per vial using quasibinomial logistic regression. The analysis tests whether ATPα genotype affects survival on cardenolide-containing Asclepias diets, whether genotype differences vary among individual host-plant diets, and whether survival declines with increasing total cardenolide concentration. 

This dataset was generated by Naama Weksler for her undergraduate thesis at UC Berkeley. Naama performed all data collection.
